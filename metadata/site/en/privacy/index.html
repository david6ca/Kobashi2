Privacy Statement

Privacy Statement · 11 November 2026

Local models process inference on your device. Model and runtime downloads contact GitHub, Microsoft package infrastructure and Hugging Face without attaching your prompts or code. Clients, plugins and tools may make their own network requests.

When you select GitHub Copilot, requests and their included conversation, code, images and tool results are sent to GitHub Copilot and its model providers under their policies and your account settings. ModelBridge is an independent product.

Accounts, credentials, settings, configuration backups and downloaded models are stored on your device. Credentials are stored in local files; ModelBridge does not claim Keychain protection or application-level encryption. Local diagnostic and setup logs record technical details and errors; review them before sharing.

Removing an account removes its saved credential from ModelBridge, not your GitHub account or data held by providers. Logs and downloaded models may remain. Quit ModelBridge before manually removing its local data; quitting first restores managed client settings.

Privacy questions: support@pomegram.com. Provider retention and deletion are governed by the selected provider.
